# Sustainable Fashion Analytics Dashboard

## Problem Statement

Analyze the environmental impact of fashion brands by consolidating data on materials, production processes, and supply chain transparency into an interactive dashboard.

## Dataset

Kaggle's Sustainable Fashion: Eco-Friendly Trends dataset with materials, production processes, eco-friendly brands, and environmental impact metrics.

## Methods

- Clean and merge datasets
- Calculate sustainability scores based on environmental and ethical metrics
- Create interactive dashboards using Plotly Dash or Tableau
- Perform correlation analysis between sustainability scores and consumer sentiment
- Identify clusters of brands by sustainability performance

## Expected Results

- Dashboard highlighting top-performing sustainable brands
- Insights on correlation between sustainability and consumer sentiment
- Guidance for retailers toward sustainable sourcing strategies

## Tech Stack

Python, Pandas, Plotly Dash or Tableau, SQL, Jupyter Notebook

## Impact

Empowers consumers and stakeholders with transparent sustainability data, promoting eco-friendly practices and encouraging brands to improve their environmental footprint.
